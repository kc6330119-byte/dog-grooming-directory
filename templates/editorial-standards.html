Editorial Standards

How we research, verify, and maintain the quality of information on {{ site_name }}

Our Commitment

Dog Groomer Locator is committed to providing accurate, helpful, and trustworthy information to dog owners seeking professional grooming services. Every listing and piece of editorial content on this site goes through a research and verification process before publication.

How We Source Listings

Groomer listings are compiled from multiple publicly available sources, including:

  • Google Business Profiles and Google Maps data
  • State and local business registrations
  • Professional grooming association directories
  • User submissions (verified before publication)

We cross-reference multiple sources to verify business names, addresses, phone numbers, and operating hours. Listings that cannot be verified through at least two independent sources are held for manual review before publication.

Editorial Content

Blog posts and guides on Dog Groomer Locator are written by authors with relevant credentials in pet care, grooming, or veterinary science. Each article includes:

  • Author attribution with professional credentials
  • Publication and last-updated dates
  • Information sourced from professional grooming standards and veterinary guidelines
  • Clear distinction between factual information and editorial opinion

Update Frequency

We review and update groomer listings on a rolling basis. Business information can change without notice — hours, services, and pricing are particularly subject to change. We encourage users to verify details directly with the grooming business before visiting. If you notice outdated or incorrect information, please let us know so we can correct it promptly.

Independence

Our listings and editorial recommendations are independent. Grooming businesses cannot pay for placement, higher rankings, or favorable reviews in our directory. Advertising and affiliate relationships are clearly disclosed and do not influence our editorial decisions or the order in which listings appear.

Corrections Policy

We take accuracy seriously. If we publish incorrect information — whether in a listing or an editorial article — we correct it as quickly as possible. Corrections are made directly in the content. If the error was significant, we note the correction at the bottom of the affected page. To report an error, use our contact form.
