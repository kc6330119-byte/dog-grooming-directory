Frequently Asked Questions

Everything pet owners need to know about dog groomers

A dog groomer is a professional who provides grooming services for dogs, including bathing, haircuts, nail trimming, ear cleaning, teeth brushing, and more. Groomers can work in full-service salons, mobile grooming vans, or as home-visit groomers. Regular grooming keeps dogs healthy, clean, and comfortable.
The frequency depends on your dog's breed, coat type, and lifestyle. Long-haired breeds often need grooming every 4–6 weeks, while short-haired dogs may only need a bath and tidy every 8–12 weeks. Nails typically need trimming every 3–4 weeks. Your groomer can recommend a schedule based on your dog's specific needs.
A full-service grooming salon is a brick-and-mortar location where you drop off your dog and pick them up later. A mobile groomer comes to your home in a self-contained grooming van, offering a one-on-one experience with no other dogs around — ideal for anxious pets. Both offer similar services, but mobile grooming typically costs more due to the convenience factor.
Look for groomers with verified reviews, proper certifications (such as National Dog Groomers Association credentials), and experience with your specific breed. Browse our Full-Service Salons or Mobile Groomers listings to find trusted professionals near you.
Dog grooming prices vary by location, dog size, coat condition, and services requested. A basic bath and brush might cost $30–$60 for a small dog, while a full groom for a large breed can run $80–$150+. Mobile groomers and premium salons tend to charge more. Use Dog Groomer Locator to browse grooming options in your area and find a price range that works for you.
Ask about their experience with your breed, what products they use, how they handle anxious dogs, and whether they require up-to-date vaccinations. It's also a good idea to ask if you can tour the facility, understand their handling methods, and clarify exactly what's included in the price quoted.
