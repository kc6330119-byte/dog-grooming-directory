Our Story

Dog Groomer Locator was built by a pet owner who got tired of the same frustrating experience: searching online for a reputable groomer in a new area, finding outdated Yelp pages, broken links, and listings with no hours, no photos, and no clear idea of what services were actually offered.

The idea was simple — build the resource that should have existed all along. A single, searchable directory covering dog groomers, grooming salons, and mobile pet groomers across the United States, with real information that helps pet owners actually plan a visit.

Today, Dog Groomer Locator is one of the most comprehensive pet grooming directories in the country, with over {{ total_count | default('2,900') }} listings across all 50 states — and growing every week.

About the Publisher

Dog Groomer Locator is built and maintained by Kevin Collins, a technology veteran with 40+ years in IT — from mainframe applications developer to enterprise data architect (including a 4-terabyte HR data warehouse serving 200,000+ users) to AI & data analytics lead at a global enterprise software company.

Kevin applies that data-engineering discipline here: every listing passes automated quality checks and verification against public business data before it is published.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make professional dog grooming easy to find for every pet owner in America. Regular grooming is essential for a dog's health and wellbeing — but only when owners can find a groomer they trust.

We believe no dog should go without proper grooming care simply because it was hard to find a qualified professional nearby. Whether you're a new pet owner looking for your first groomer, or a seasoned dog parent who just moved to a new city, Dog Groomer Locator is here to help.

What We List

Our directory covers the full spectrum of professional dog grooming — from small independent groomers operating out of a home studio to large franchise salons and mobile grooming services.

  • ✂️
    Full-Service Grooming Salons — Drop-off facilities offering complete grooming packages including bath, haircut, nail trim, ear cleaning, and more. Great for regular appointments.
  • 🚐
    Mobile Groomers — Self-contained grooming vans that come directly to your home. Ideal for anxious dogs or pet owners with busy schedules who prefer the convenience.
  • 🏠
    Home-Based Groomers — Independent groomers who operate from a personal studio, often offering a quieter, more one-on-one experience for dogs who don't do well in busy salon environments.
  • 🐕
    Breed Specialists — Groomers with specialized expertise in specific breeds or coat types, including doodles, double-coated breeds, and dogs requiring hand-stripping or specialized cuts.
