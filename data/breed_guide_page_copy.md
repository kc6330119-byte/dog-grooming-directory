## Page Intro Copy

### Hero / Intro Section (for `/dog-grooming-guide.html`)

**Heading:** The Dog Grooming Guide by Breed

**Subheading:** Find your breed. Learn what it needs. Connect with a groomer who knows how to deliver it.

---

Every dog deserves a grooming routine that actually fits their coat — not a one-size-fits-all schedule copied from a pet care forum. A Siberian Husky and a Bichon Frise both need regular professional grooming, but the frequency, technique, and tools are completely different. Getting this right makes grooming appointments less stressful for your dog and more cost-effective for you.

We've assembled grooming profiles for **67 breeds**, organized by coat type so you can understand not just *what* to do, but *why* — and how your dog's coat dictates everything from how often you need to brush to which type of groomer has the right expertise for your specific breed. Each profile now includes shedding level, hypoallergenic status, bathing frequency, and professional grooming guidance so you can plan your dog's care routine with confidence.

Use the guide below to find your breed, learn its grooming needs, and when you're ready, find a local groomer who specializes in exactly what your dog requires.

### Section Intro Labels (for coat-type filter tabs)

**Short Coats** — Minimal brushing, but don't skip nail care and breed-specific attention (folds, ears, skin).

**Curly Coats** — Daily brushing is non-negotiable. These coats mat fast and need professional trims every 4–8 weeks.

**Double Coats** — Plan for seasonal shedding. Regular deshedding and professional baths make a dramatic difference.

**Long Coats** — Beautiful and high-effort. Daily brushing keeps tangles at bay between professional appointments every 4–6 weeks.

**Wire Coats** — Seek out a groomer who hand-strips. Clipping gets the job done but changes the coat over time.

**Hairless** — Skincare, not coat care. Daily moisturizer and sunscreen replace daily brushing.

**Silky Coats** — Feathering mats easily. Brush 3–4 times per week and keep up with ear care.
