Reviewed against the **rendered build**, sampled per protocol: homepage, 3 state hubs
(new-hampshire, vermont, ohio), 8 city hubs incl. the 3 smallest indexable, 500 randomly
sampled listing pages across the index/noindex split, all 6 policy/trust pages, category
and blog pages.

## Findings

### Finding 1 — Machine-composed added value [Major, P1/P2]
**Evidence:** 2,695 listing descriptions were generated by an LLM on 2026-07-01
(`website_descriptions.py`); the remainder are template-composed from Airtable fields
(`generate_fact_descriptions.py`). Example, `dist/groomer/doggie-styling-colorado-springs.html`:
*"…operated by owner and groomer Veta Guerra, who brings over 20 years of experience…"*

**Why a reviewer flags it:** P1 requires *"additional commentary, curation, or otherwise
adding value"* — the natural reading is publisher-added value. P2 names *"copying content
then modifying it slightly via automated techniques"* as scraping. A reviewer cannot
distinguish "AI summarized the business's own About page" from "AI spun the scraped record."

**Counter-evidence (genuine, and it matters):** the source is the business's *own website* —
a second, independent source — not the scraped Google record. The facts surfaced (owner
names, price points, deposit policies, payment methods, years operating) are absent from
the Google Maps result. Measured duplication is ~0 and max pairwise similarity is 0.59.
This is materially different from the spintax layer that caused the original rejection.

**Disposition:** Standing finding, retained live by operator decision. **No further
machine prose will be generated during this loop** (Hard Constraint 1).

**Fix (requires human):** first-party curation on a subset — see FINAL-REPORT checklist.

---

### Finding 2 — 1,522 pages claim a review process the site does not perform [Blocker, P4]
**Evidence:** `templates/groomer.html:54` renders, on 1,522 pages:
> "This dog groomer listing is being reviewed for accuracy before it appears in the public directory."

and `build.py:869-871` emits the matching meta description
*"…is being reviewed for accuracy."*

**Why a reviewer flags it:** P4 forbids *"claiming content or services the site does not
actually provide."* No human reviews these listings. They failed an **automated** quality
gate (`description_quality_check`) and, absent a data change, will never "appear in the
public directory." The sentence asserts an active editorial process and a temporary state;
both are false. This is the single most indefensible thing on the site — it is a written,
verifiable false claim, not a matter of interpretation.

**Fix:** remove the claim. Stop emitting the stub entirely (see Finding 3).

---

### Finding 3 — 1,522 under-construction screens [Blocker, P1/P2/P3]
**Evidence:** median **30 body words**. Sample `dist/groomer/shampoodle-wichita.html`:
*"Listing under review | Shampoodle | This dog groomer listing is being reviewed…"*

**Why a reviewer flags it:** P1 explicitly bars screens *"without publisher content or with
low-value content, under construction."* These are textbook under-construction screens.
They are noindexed and unlinked from hubs, but they are live, crawlable URLs and Google
currently reports ~10.6K indexed pages against an intended ~3.9K — meaning a large share of
this stub surface is still in the index. P2's remedy for many-similar-pages is *expand or
consolidate*; these cannot be expanded (the underlying records lack the data — Services is
17.2% filled, Specialties 11.4%), so they must be consolidated away.

**Verified safe to remove:** overlap between the 1,522 stubs and the 396 GSC click-earning
protected slugs is **0**. No traffic-earning URL is affected.

**Fix:** stop emitting stub HTML; return HTTP 410 Gone.

---

### Finding 4 — 481 noindexed city hubs are thin listing fragments [Minor, P2]
**Evidence:** `dist/state/missouri/ballwin.html` — 83 words, two listing rows, no editorial.
**Why:** thin, but correctly noindexed, genuinely useful to a user who lands there, and not
a doorway (similarity 0.093). Consolidation is defensible later; not a blocker.

---

## Verdict — reviewer's voice

**REJECT.**

The two blockers I was told to assume are, on the evidence, not what sinks this site. The
listing pages carry a median of 355 unique words with effectively zero duplication, and the
location hubs are not doorways — a state hub runs 1,324 median words of hand-written
editorial and no two hubs share more than 9% of their shingles. That work is real.

What sinks it is simpler and worse: **1,522 pages tell the visitor that a human is reviewing
the listing for accuracy, and no human is.** The site is asserting an editorial service it
does not provide, on roughly a quarter of its URLs, in plain English. Under P4 that is a
false claim; under P1 those same screens are under-construction placeholders with thirty
words on them. I do not need to adjudicate whether machine-written descriptions constitute
"curation" to reject this site — I can point at a sentence that is not true.

**Single most damaging reason:** 1,522 URLs claim an accuracy-review process that does not
exist (P4), rendered as 30-word under-construction screens (P1).
